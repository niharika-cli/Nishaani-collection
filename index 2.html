What "nishaani" means to us

A nishaani is a mark — a small token someone keeps to remember a person, a place, a moment. That's the feeling we make jewellery for.

Oxidized silver already carries that feeling in the metal itself. It darkens and settles the way memory does — nothing about it looks factory-fresh, and that's the point.

We're starting with jhumkas, because a good pair says something the moment you put them on — at a wedding, or with an ordinary Tuesday outfit.
